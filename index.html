Made for real invoices

Reads invoices in English, Arabic, Kurdish, German and French, in £, €, $ and IQD – any column order, photos or PDFs. Each supplier can have its own currency, and amounts are never mixed. The app itself is available in all five languages, with right-to-left layout for Arabic and Kurdish.
