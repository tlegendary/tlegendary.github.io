Common questions

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or tap Manage or cancel subscription in the app's Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

I changed phone – how do I get my subscription back?

Open ChargeCheck and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

How do I move my prices and reports to a new phone?

In the app go to Settings › Save backup file, save it to Files or iCloud Drive, then on the new phone choose Settings › Restore backup › Choose a backup file. The app also keeps automatic daily backups in Files › On My iPhone › ChargeCheck.

An invoice was read wrongly

Take the photo flat, close and in good light, or upload the PDF if your supplier emails one. Anything the app isn't sure about is shown to you to confirm, and you can edit any line.

How does the free version work?

Watch one short ad to scan one invoice. Subscribers get unlimited scans with no ads.
