Scanning and checking

How do I check an invoice?
  1. Add your agreed prices in More › Price list (type them, paste a list or import a spreadsheet).
  2. Tap Scan an invoice on Home and take a photo, choose one from Photos, or open a PDF.
  3. ChargeCheck compares every line with your prices and shows what you were overcharged.

Try the sample invoice in the app first to see how it works.

An invoice was read wrongly

Lay the invoice flat, fill the screen, and use good light without shadows. If your supplier emails PDFs, open the PDF instead – it's read exactly.

Lines the app isn't sure about are shaded amber. Tap any line to correct it; ChargeCheck remembers how each supplier names an item for next time.

The same item has a different name on each invoice

Open the item in More › Price list and add its other names under On invoices as. Every name then matches the same price.

Which languages and currencies can it read?

Invoices in English, Arabic, Kurdish, German and French, in £, €, $ or IQD. If a supplier invoices in a different currency from yours, open the supplier and set Invoices in – their amounts are then kept separate and never added to your own currency.

Something was missing from my delivery

Open the invoice and tap Check the delivery PACK. Tick off what arrived; anything short is taken off what you pay and added to the credit request for the supplier.

Does ChargeCheck need the internet?

No. Invoices are read on your iPhone and checking works offline. The internet is only needed for purchases, ads in the free version, and sharing with staff.

Suppliers and payments

How do I record a payment to a supplier?

Open Suppliers, tap the supplier and choose Record a payment. Enter any amount, how you paid (cash, transfer, card or cheque), a reference and a photo of the receipt. It pays off their oldest invoices first – or use Pay invoices one by one to choose exactly which.

How do I get money back for an overcharge?

Open the invoice and send the credit request – as a message, or as a PDF PACK. When the supplier's credit note arrives, scan it or add it from the supplier's page; it comes off what you owe them.

What is a trusted supplier?

For suppliers you don't need to price-check, turn on Trusted. Scanning their invoice just reads the total and adds it to what you owe them.

Can I see when invoices are due?

Yes PRO. On the supplier's page set Pay within (for example 30 days). Overdue invoices and the cash you need this week then show on Home.

I deleted something by mistake

Invoices, payments and prices you delete stay in More › Recently deleted for 30 days. Swipe right on one to restore it.

How do I cancel or change my subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings › your name › Subscriptions, choose ChargeCheck, then change or cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Is there a free trial?

Pro starts with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. If you cancel during the trial you aren't charged.

I changed phone – how do I get my subscription back?

Open ChargeCheck, go to the plans screen and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

Do scan packs expire?

No. Scans you buy stay until you use them, and the pack features stay unlocked for good – even after the scans run out. They move to a new phone with a backup file.

How do I ask for a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com, signed in with your Apple ID.

Your data, backups and staff

Where are my invoices kept?

On your iPhone. ChargeCheck has no account and no server: your invoices, prices and payments never leave your phone unless you share them or turn on staff sharing, which uses your own iCloud. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I move everything to a new phone?
  1. On the old phone open More › Settings › Save backup file and save it to iCloud Drive or Files.
  2. On the new phone open More › Settings › Restore backup and choose that file.

ChargeCheck also keeps automatic backups for the last 14 days in Files › On My iPhone › ChargeCheck.

Can my staff check invoices on their phones?

Yes PRO. Open More › Staff and invite them. Staff scan and check invoices on their own iPhones; everything comes to yours through iCloud. Staff don't see payments or your price list.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone – this removes everything stored by ChargeCheck. Backup files you saved yourself stay in Files until you delete them. If you shared with staff, stop sharing in More › Staff first to remove the shared copy from your iCloud.
