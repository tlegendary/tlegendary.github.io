Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

ChargeCheck (“the app”) is provided by RJ. Your privacy matters, so the app is built to keep your information on your own device.

What the app uses

  • Invoice photos and PDFs you choose. They are read on your iPhone to find the items and prices. They are not uploaded or sent to us or anyone else.
  • Camera and photo library, only when you choose to take or pick an invoice photo.
  • Your price lists, shops, invoice checks and settings, stored only on your device, including automatic backup files in the app's own folder in the Files app.

What we collect

We (RJ) do not collect or store any personal data. The app has no account and no analytics. Your invoices, prices and reports never leave your device unless you choose to share or export them.

Advertising in the free version

If you choose the free version, you can watch a short video ad to scan an invoice. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show an ad, Google may collect information from your device such as an advertising or device identifier, your approximate location (from your IP address), and how you interact with the ad. Google uses this information to deliver, measure and limit ads, and in the UK and EU it asks for your consent first where required; you can change that choice later under Settings › Ad privacy choices in the app. Google's use of this data is covered by its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and your invoice contents are never shared with Google. Subscribers see no ads.

Payments

Subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment under Apple's own privacy policy. We do not receive your card details.

Files you export or share

PDF reports, spreadsheets, backups and credit requests are only sent where you choose (for example Files, Mail or WhatsApp), under that service's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all of its data and its backup folder. Copies you saved elsewhere stay where you saved them. You can also clear prices and reports inside the app.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children. Ads are not intended for children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: RJ, r93.hamad@gmail.com.

Terms of Use

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
