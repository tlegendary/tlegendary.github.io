Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

ChargeCheck (“the app”) is provided by RJ. The app is built to keep your business information on your own device.

What the app uses

  • Invoice photos and PDFs you choose. They are read on your iPhone to find the items and prices. They are not uploaded or sent to us or anyone else.
  • Camera and photo library, only when you choose to take or pick a photo of an invoice or a payment receipt.
  • Your price lists, suppliers, payments, receipt photos, shops, invoice checks and settings, stored only on your device. This includes automatic backup files in the app's own folder in the Files app, items you deleted (kept for 30 days so you can restore them), and the history of changes made in the app.
  • Home screen widget. If you add it, the widget shows what you owe your suppliers. Its figures are kept on your device.

What we collect

We (RJ) do not collect or store any personal data. The app has no account and no analytics. Your invoices, prices, payments and reports never leave your device unless you choose to share, export or sync them as described below.

Apple Intelligence

On iPhones that support it, the app can ask Apple Intelligence to double-check names and matches, find an invoice number, or write your monthly summary. This runs on your iPhone using Apple's on-device model; nothing is sent to us.

Sharing with your staff

If you turn on staff sharing, your invoice checks, prices, payments and the related settings are stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit and shared only with the people you invite. Staff phones store what is shared with them. We have no access to this data. Apple handles it under its own privacy policy. You can stop sharing at any time in the app under More › Staff, which removes the shared copy.

Advertising in the free version

In the free version you can watch a short video ad to scan an invoice. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show an ad, Google may collect information from your device such as an advertising or device identifier, your approximate location (from your IP address), and how you interact with the ad. Google uses this information to deliver, measure and limit ads, and in the UK and EU it asks for your consent first where required; you can change that choice later under More › Settings › Ad privacy choices. Google's use of this data is covered by its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and your invoice contents are never shared with Google. Subscribers and anyone who has bought scans see no ads.

Payments

Subscriptions and scan packs are bought and managed through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment under Apple's own privacy policy. We do not receive your name, Apple ID or card details.

Files you export or share

PDF reports, spreadsheets, backups, statements and credit requests are only sent where you choose (for example Files, iCloud Drive, Mail or WhatsApp), under that service's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app deletes all of its data and its backup folder from your iPhone. If you shared with staff, stop sharing first to remove the shared copy from your iCloud. Copies you saved elsewhere stay where you saved them. You can also delete invoices, payments and prices inside the app; they are removed for good after 30 days in Recently deleted, or straight away if you delete them from there.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children. Ads are not intended for children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: RJ, r93.hamad@gmail.com.

Terms of Use

Last updated 14 November 2026

Use of the app is governed by Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

Subscriptions

ChargeCheck Pro is available as a monthly or yearly subscription, with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase, or at the end of the free trial. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone's Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Scan packs

Scan packs are one-off purchases. Scans you buy do not expire, and the features that come with scan packs stay unlocked.

Checking figures

The app helps you compare invoices with prices you enter. Reading photos is never perfect, so always check important figures against the original invoice before relying on them or asking a supplier for money back.
